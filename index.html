Who We Are

Ghana's
National Body
for Powerlifting

The Ghana Association of Powerlifting (G.A.P.) is the official national governing body for the sport of powerlifting in Ghana. Founded in 2026, G.A.P. operates as a not-for-profit organisation dedicated to developing the sport at every level — from grassroots participation to international competition.

G.A.P. is affiliated to the International Powerlifting Federation (IPF) and the African Powerlifting Federation (APF), ensuring Ghana's athletes compete on the world stage under internationally recognised rules and standards. All G.A.P. events follow current IPF Technical Rules with full WADA-compliant anti-doping protocols.

📜 Governance Structure

G.A.P. is governed by three legal bodies established under its Constitution. The General Assembly is the supreme governing body, meeting annually no less than one day before the National Championship. The Executive Committee functions as the Board of Directors between General Assemblies, holding fiduciary responsibility. All governance structures comply with the IPF Constitution, By-Laws, and IOC Basic Universal Principles of Good Governance.

GA
General Assembly — Supreme governing body, meets annually
EC
Executive Committee — Board of Directors, manages day-to-day governance
CO
Commissions — Technical, Anti-Doping, Disciplinary, Appeals

⚖️ IPF Compliance

G.A.P. operates in full compliance with the IPF Constitution, IPF By-Laws, IPF Technical Rules, and the IPF Anti-Doping Rules. All members are bound by the World Anti-Doping Code (WADA). The Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS) in Lausanne is recognised as the sole external jurisdiction body for disputes. G.A.P. is not affiliated with any organisation with similar objectives to the IPF or any organisation purporting to be a worldwide governing body for powerlifting.

Eligibility,
Membership &
National Team

G.A.P. membership and athlete eligibility are governed by the G.A.P. Constitution (Article IV) and IPF By-Laws. All athletes competing in G.A.P. events must be current, financial members and comply with IPF Anti-Doping Rules.

Eligibility Requirements
Ghanaian citizens, Ghanaian residents, persons of Ghanaian descent, or individuals otherwise approved by the Executive Committee consistent with IPF eligibility rules (IPF By-Law 14.9).
Membership Types
Full Membership — Athlete, coach, and referee participation rights.
Coach / Referee Membership — Coach and/or referee capacities only.
IPF-Affiliated Guest — Members of other IPF nations may participate in G.A.P. events.
National Team Selection
National team selection for APF and IPF international championships will be based on performance at the G.A.P. National Championships and compliance with IPF qualifying totals. Athletes must hold a valid Ghanaian passport or demonstrate six months of verified residency per IPF By-Law 14.9.
International Competition Registration
Athletes selected for international representation register through G.A.P. Entry, travel, and accommodation coordination handled by the Secretary-General. A registration portal for international events will be available once G.A.P. achieves IPF provisional membership.

Compliance

IPF Affiliated
Federation

G.A.P. operates under the framework of the International Powerlifting Federation, the world governing body for the sport. All G.A.P. competitions, records, athlete eligibility, and anti-doping standards are governed by IPF rules and the World Anti-Doping Code.

  • Constitution and By-Laws adopted in conformity with IPF Constitution and By-Laws
  • All competitions conducted under IPF Technical Rules (current edition)
  • Full WADA-code compliant anti-doping programme per IPF Anti-Doping Rules
  • Athletes bound by IPF Anti-Doping Rules as a condition of membership and participation
  • Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS) recognised as sole external jurisdiction body
  • African Powerlifting Federation (APF) — Regional affiliation pending IPF provisional membership
  • Not affiliated with any competing international association per IPF By-Law 12.3
  • General Assembly minutes and financial reports published publicly for transparency
  • Annual calendar includes Classic (Raw) National Championship and Equipped National Championship
