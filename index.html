Who We Are

Setting the Standard for Strength in Ghana

The Ghana Association of Powerlifting (G.A.P.) is the official national governing body for the sport of powerlifting in Ghana. Founded in 2026, G.A.P. operates as a not-for-profit organisation dedicated to developing the sport from grassroots to international competition.

Affiliated with the International Powerlifting Federation (IPF) and the African Powerlifting Federation (APF), we ensure our athletes compete on the world stage under internationally recognised rules and full WADA-compliant anti-doping protocols.

🏆 Competition

National Championships and regional qualifiers run under strict IPF Technical Rules.

🧪 Clean Sport

Absolute commitment to WADA-code compliance and anti-doping testing.

🎓 Education

World-class referee certification and athlete development programmes.

🌍 International

Clear pathways for Ghanaian athletes to the APF and IPF World stages.
