Who we are

Ghana's national body for powerlifting.

The Black Star Powerlifting Association (BSPA), formerly the Ghana Association of Powerlifting (G.A.P.), is the official national governing body for the sport of powerlifting in Ghana. Founded in 2026, BSPA operates as a not-for-profit organisation dedicated to developing the sport at every level — from grassroots participation to international competition.

BSPA is affiliated to the International Powerlifting Federation (IPF) and the African Powerlifting Federation (APF), ensuring Ghana's athletes compete under internationally recognised rules and standards. All BSPA events follow current IPF Technical Rules with full WADA-compliant anti-doping protocols.

Governance & transparency

Constitution, by-laws & official documents.

BSPA is governed by three bodies established under its Constitution, in line with the IPF Constitution and By-Laws and the IOC Basic Universal Principles of Good Governance.

GAGeneral Assembly — the supreme governing body, meeting annually no less than one day before the National Championship.
ECExecutive Committee — acts as the Board of Directors between General Assemblies, with fiduciary responsibility.
COCommissions — Technical, Anti-Doping, Disciplinary and Appeals.
IPF compliance

BSPA complies with the IPF Constitution, By-Laws, Technical Rules and Anti-Doping Rules. All members are bound by the World Anti-Doping Code. The Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS) in Lausanne is recognised as the sole external jurisdiction body for disputes. BSPA is not affiliated with any organisation purporting to be a worldwide governing body for powerlifting other than the IPF.

Membership

Eligibility, membership & national team.

Membership and athlete eligibility are governed by the BSPA Constitution (Article IV) and the IPF By-Laws. Athletes competing in BSPA events must be current, financial members and comply with the IPF Anti-Doping Rules.

Eligibility requirements

Ghanaian citizens, Ghanaian residents, persons of Ghanaian descent, or individuals otherwise approved by the Executive Committee consistent with IPF eligibility rules (IPF By-Law 14.9).

Membership types

Full membership — athlete, coach and referee participation rights. Coach / referee membership — coach and/or referee capacities only. IPF-affiliated guest — members of other IPF nations may participate in BSPA events.

National team selection

Selection for APF and IPF international championships is based on performance at BSPA National Championships and IPF qualifying totals. Athletes must hold a valid Ghanaian passport or demonstrate six months of verified residency per IPF By-Law 14.9.

International competition registration

Athletes selected for international representation register through BSPA; entry, travel and accommodation are coordinated by the Secretary-General. An international events portal will open once BSPA achieves IPF provisional membership.

Compliance

Operating under the IPF framework.

All BSPA competitions, records, athlete eligibility and anti-doping standards are governed by the rules of the International Powerlifting Federation and the World Anti-Doping Code.

  • Constitution and By-Laws conform to the IPF Constitution and By-Laws
  • All competitions run under current IPF Technical Rules
  • WADA Code–compliant anti-doping programme
  • Athletes bound by IPF Anti-Doping Rules as a condition of membership
  • CAS recognised as sole external jurisdiction body
  • APF regional affiliation pending IPF provisional membership
  • No affiliation with competing international associations
  • Minutes and financial reports published publicly
